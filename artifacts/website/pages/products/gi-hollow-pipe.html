PRODUCT OVERVIEW

About GI Hollow Pipes

GI (Galvanized Iron) Hollow Pipes are hot-dip galvanized steel hollow sections manufactured to meet stringent international quality standards. The zinc coating provides excellent corrosion resistance, ideal for indoor and outdoor construction.

Available in square, rectangular, and round profiles, sourced directly from certified Chinese steel manufacturers. Every batch meets specified standards before shipment.

Our GI Hollow Pipes are used across Myanmar for structural frameworks, industrial piping, fencing, carports, and general construction.

Superior Corrosion ResistanceHot-dip zinc coating (min. 85 g/m²) provides long-lasting rust protection.
Wide Size RangeSquare from 20×20mm to 150×150mm; rectangular from 25×50mm to 100×200mm.
International StandardsManufactured to GB/T 3091 and ASTM A53 with available mill certificates.
Factory Direct WholesaleDirect from certified Chinese manufacturers to Myanmar. Contact for bulk pricing.
Precision Dimensional ControlTight tolerances on wall thickness and dimensions ensure consistent performance.
