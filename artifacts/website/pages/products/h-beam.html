About H Beams

H-beams (wide-flange or W-beams) deliver exceptional load-bearing capacity, making them the backbone of heavy construction projects.

Sourced from certified Chinese steel mills to GB/T 11263 and EN 10034 standards. Q235B for standard and Q345B for high-strength applications.

H-beams are used across Myanmar for building frames, bridge girders, crane runways, and industrial sheds.

High Load-Bearing CapacityWide flange design distributes loads efficiently for superior structural performance.
Q235B & Q345B GradesQ235B for general use; Q345B high-yield for demanding structural applications.
Wide Size RangeFlange widths 100mm–400mm, web heights 100mm–900mm. Standard 6m & 12m lengths.
Mill CertifiedMaterial test reports and mill certificates available for full traceability.
Factory-Direct PricingDirect from certified Chinese steel mills to Myanmar. Contact for bulk quotation.
