About Our Glass Sheets

AlbA Trading supplies premium float glass and tempered safety glass from certified Chinese manufacturers, meeting international quality standards.

Float glass gives optical clarity for windows and interiors. Tempered glass is 4-5× stronger with safe breakage patterns.

We offer clear, tinted (Bronze, Blue, Green, Grey), and reflective glass. Custom cutting available.

High Optical Clarity88%–92% light transmittance for maximum natural light.
Multiple Thickness OptionsAvailable in 3mm, 4mm, 5mm, 6mm, 8mm, 10mm, and 12mm.
Tempered Safety GlassHeat-treated tempered glass is 4-5× stronger and breaks safely into granules.
International StandardsManufactured to GB 11614 and EN 572 standards.
