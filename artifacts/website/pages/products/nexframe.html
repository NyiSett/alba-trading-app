PRODUCT OVERVIEW

About NEXFRAME™ uPVC

NEXFRAME is AlbA Trading's exclusive uPVC sliding window and door profile system. Manufactured to exacting standards, designed for Myanmar's tropical and monsoon climate.

uPVC profiles offer superior advantages: better thermal insulation, sound dampening, no corrosion, minimal maintenance, and elegant finish options.

NEXFRAME suits residential villas, condominiums, commercial buildings, hotels, and industrial facilities.

Superior Thermal InsulationMulti-chamber profiles provide excellent thermal break, reducing heat transfer.
Weather Seal EPDM StripsEPDM rubber seals provide excellent water-tightness and dust resistance.
Zero MaintenanceuPVC never corrodes, warps, or requires painting. Wipe clean, zero maintenance.
Custom Sizes AvailableFabricated to exact project dimensions. Standard and custom configurations available.
Multiple Color OptionsAvailable in White, Brown, Grey, Champagne, and custom colors.
