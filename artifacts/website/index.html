About AlbA Trading Co., Ltd

AlbA Trading Co., Ltd is a professional construction materials trading company headquartered at the Myawaddy border trade hub in Kayin State, Myanmar. We specialize in sourcing premium construction materials directly from certified manufacturers in China.
